What is shilajit

Shilajit is a black, sticky resin found in the rocks of the Himalayas. It forms over centuries from decomposed plants pressed into the mountain. The highest grade comes from above 17,000 feet.

It carries over 85 minerals plus fulvic and humic acid. A pea-sized portion in warm milk or water is all you need.

  • Gold gradeSun-dried, never heat processed
  • CleanNo heavy metals, chemicals or microbes
  • One ingredientShilajit resin. Nothing else.
